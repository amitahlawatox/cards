# Bespoke Card Studio Global SEO Taxonomy and 12-Month Build Order

## Objective

Build `bespokecardstudio.com` into a global invitation search property that covers major occasions, global festivals, national celebrations, religious events, and community-specific invitation needs without narrowing the brand to one country or one cultural group.

Primary 24-36 month goal:

- `10,000 organic sessions/day`

Primary ads-only operating goal:

- maximize indexed pages with real search demand
- maximize image-search visibility
- maximize pageviews per session through dense internal linking

## Operating Principles

1. The brand stays global.
2. Country-, religion-, and community-specific pages are expansion layers, not the whole site identity.
3. We do not publish every possible event blindly.
4. New clusters must be chosen by demand, reuse potential, and monetizable traffic value.
5. Indexation quality matters more than raw page count.

## Global Content Model

The site should be organized in four layers.

### Layer 1: Evergreen Global Occasion Hubs

These are the base traffic engine and should always be the strongest clusters:

- wedding

- anniversary

## Event Prioritization Framework

Each new event cluster should be scored across five factors:

1. invitation intent strength
2. global or multi-market reach
3. template reuse potential
4. image-search potential
5. internal-linking fit with existing clusters

Build only if the event scores well on at least three of the five.

## 12-Month Build Order

### Months 1-3

Primary goal:

- stabilize indexation
- expand the highest-value global and seasonal gaps

Build order:

- 4th of July
- Thanksgiving
- Mother's Day
- Father's Day
- Valentine's Day strengthening
- Christmas strengthening
- Lunar New Year
- Easter

Content output target:

- `8` new event hubs
- `32-48` modifier pages across those hubs
- `16-24` editorial/wording pages
- `24+` upgraded template-detail pages

Technical target:

- reduce orphan-page count sharply
- reduce long-title count
- ensure every new page has direct internal links from at least `3` indexed pages

### Months 4-6

Primary goal:

- expand major global and festival coverage
- deepen pages that already get impressions

Build order:

- Diwali strengthening
- Eid strengthening
- Holi
- Navratri
- Pride events
- school open house
- back to school
- fundraiser / gala

Content output target:

- `8` more cluster hubs
- `24-40` new editorial pages
- `30+` upgraded template pages

### Months 7-9

Primary goal:

- add region-aware national-event coverage
- build reusable country-event patterns

Build order:

- Canada Day
- Australia Day
- Independence Day India
- Independence Day Pakistan
- Super Bowl / watch party
- office holiday party
- church event invitations
- temple event invitations

Constraint:

- only expand country-event pages where templates, wording, and seasonal relevance can be reused

### Months 10-12

Primary goal:

- consolidate winners
- prune weak pages
- expand the highest-performing markets and event types

Build order depends on GSC winners:

- top-performing holiday clusters
- top-performing wedding/family clusters
- highest-CTR editorial formats
- strongest image-search template categories

## Monthly Publishing Cadence

Sustainable target:

- `6-10` new event or seasonal hub pages
- `12-20` new editorial pages
- `20-40` refreshed template-detail pages
- `1` internal-link cleanup pass
- `1` title/meta cleanup pass
- `1` Search Console indexation review

## Page Types to Scale Aggressively

These page types are most likely to support an ads-only growth model:

- wording pages
- message-template pages
- send-timing pages
- event-planning pages
- comparison pages
- holiday/seasonal hubs
- image-rich template detail pages
- printable and photo-intent pages

## Page Types to Treat Carefully

These can create indexing debt if scaled too fast:

- low-differentiation modifier pages
- pages with nearly identical intros and FAQs
- narrow local pages with weak search intent
- AI-generated pages with no unique examples or utility

## Success Criteria

By month 12:

- strong indexation improvement on new clusters
- at least `100-150` pages with recurring impressions
- multiple clusters consistently ranking in positions `10-30`
- stronger image-search pickup

By month 24:

- `3-5` major clusters driving most of the traffic
- consistent seasonal spikes supported by evergreen traffic
- clear global spread across US, UK, Canada, Australia, India, and other English-friendly markets

By month 36:

- `10,000 organic sessions/day` from a blend of evergreen occasions, seasonal events, cultural festivals, and editorial helper pages
